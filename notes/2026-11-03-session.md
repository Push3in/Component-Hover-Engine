## 1. Card sizing (DialKit `maxScale`)

- `maxScale` default **2.25** (slider now 0.5–4), in `src/App.jsx`.
- Card size = the smaller of the two: *fit to 75% of the window* (`fill`) vs the *maxScale cap*. If the cap is above the fit, moving it does nothing. To make cards bigger on a normal screen, raise **fill**.
- After changing a default in code, press **Cmd+R**: DialKit keeps the old slider value across hot reloads.

## 2. Player card (2nd): plays by itself

- `src/usePlayback.js`: a `requestAnimationFrame` clock (2:30 song, loops) that writes `--progress` (0–1) onto the track.
- Dot and played fill move with **transforms** (`translate` / `scale`), not `left` / `width`, so they glide by sub-pixels instead of jumping 1 px at a time.
- Time labels count up / down once a second (tabular numbers so they don't jitter).
- **Play / pause button** (`src/assets/pause.svg` is a placeholder icon; swap in a Figma one).

## 3. Status card (middle): staggered spring build-in

- Title **Sleep**, description **sleep pattern of two weeks**.
- Every piece springs in one after another (title → description → 9 Comments → 10% → 12 heatmap cells → slider → labels).
- Loops: build in → hold 3 s → clear away (bottom first) → repeat.
- Waits until all cards have spread out on load (`src/introContext.js`), skips the first clear-away.
- Tune in `INTRO` / `LOOP` at the top of `src/StatusCard.jsx`.

## 4. Page-load intro: pile in the middle, then spread

- `src/useIntro.js`: an extra layer inside each card starts with the **opposite** of its fan transform (so it sits in the middle), then springs to zero.
- The start pose is written to the DOM before first paint (fixed the "flash of the finished fan").
- All cards pop in together (`appearStagger: 0`), hold, then spread. Dials in the **intro** folder (reload to see changes).

## 5. Audience card (4th): knob drags itself

- `src/AudienceCard.jsx`: knob eases to random spots (`DRAG.speed: 16` s per full track, i.e. slowed to 0.25×).
- Positive / Neutral / Negative follow the knob proportionally (always 100%); headline shows the biggest.
- Tried rolling digits and removed them again (you preferred plain numbers).

## 6. Slow motion dial

What we learned along the way:
- Style references came from your Atlas **Interaction**, **Interface (Savee)** and **X** videos: one component on a soft light background, a slow deliberate cursor, and camera moves that **punch in with an ease-out** (full speed at the start, long soft landing), like the "Review your new plan" clip.
- The ribbon looked chaotic when the cursor hovered too fast (the push physics treats it as a swipe). Hovers are now slow; one fast swipe is the only push.
- The DialKit panel looked **duplicated** when we dissolved between a full view and a split view of the same moment: see [`screenshots/dialkit-duplicate-1-small-panel.png`](screenshots/dialkit-duplicate-1-small-panel.png) and [`screenshots/dialkit-duplicate-2-big-panel.png`](screenshots/dialkit-duplicate-2-big-panel.png). Fixed by using one moving camera instead of a split.
- Dissolve between the two recordings is **0.1 s**.
